# RAM Construction Charleston · direction card

**Direction: Fanlight** (pass 01, 2026-09-25, 88) → **Fanlight II · The Arcade** (pass 02, same day).

Pass 01 was drafted green-led, recoloured to Black + Amber Red on Rohan's instruction (*"keep our Black, Amber Red
theme design and layout"*), then reviewed against the amber-red Charlotte build (O-Group Luxury, local `src/`):
*"I like the image and the messaging … I loved the images and arches, I just didn't like the placements of them.
I didn't like the header or the footer … they lack the sophistication we need in million-dollar homes."*

So pass 02 keeps every photograph, every arch and every line of copy, and rebuilds the placements: the arches
stop being card thumbnails and become architecture (a pair of fanlights, an arcade, a staggered colonnade), the
boxes go (no lifted cards, no rounded tiles, no boxed FAQ, no badge), the header becomes a masthead and the
footer a colophon.

**Footer (colophon):** sign-off row (lockup + a fan glazing + one serif line), four ruled columns with the
arch-marked small-caps heads (Charleston line with the number at display size, What we build, Where we build,
The practice), base row with licence and image provenance. Charlotte's footer is a centred stack.

**Distinct from Charlotte (O-Group Luxury):** serif vs condensed caps, arches vs hard edges, warm golden hour vs
monochrome daylight, light masthead on scroll vs black bar, ruled ledgers vs bordered cards.

**Distinct from Palmetto Painting (the other Charleston system):** no haint blue, no pink, no Young Serif, no paint chips.

Budget: desktop ≤ 10,500 px, visible words ≤ 1,300, no stretch over 2.5 screens without an ask.
